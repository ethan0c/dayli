## Description (4000 max; this is 1,540)
```
Semli is the daily planner for students who also work. Drop in a syllabus or a screenshot of your shift schedule, and Semli reads the dates for you. Then it tells you what matters today.

Set up a semester in two minutes
Add a syllabus PDF or a screenshot from class. Semli finds every lecture, exam, quiz, and due date, and puts them on your calendar with the course attached. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Screenshot your shifts
Work schedules come as photos, PDFs, and group-chat messages. Send Semli any of them. When the schedule changes, send the new one: Semli shows which shifts are new, moved, or dropped before it changes anything.

Know what matters today
Today opens on the next thing, a bar of your day, and the list under it. A red mark means two things overlap. Semli warns you before a study group lands on a shift.

One calm week
Classes, shifts, and life in one place. Green is done, red is an overlap, a ring is an exam. Swipe a row to complete it, swipe the other way to delete it, undo from the toast.

Reminders that fit a student's day
A morning briefing, a nudge before each shift and class, and an alarm for anything you mark urgent.

Free, with Plus for the heavy lifting
The calendar, to-dos, reminders, and overlap warnings are free, always. Semli Plus adds unlimited imports, Canvas sync, updates to a schedule you already imported, and suggested times when plans collide. Try it free for 7 days.

Private by default
No account needed. Your plan lives on your phone. Screenshots and files are read once to extract dates and are not stored.

Made for college students, including nursing students juggling clinicals and shifts.

Terms: https://semli.app/terms
Privacy: https://semli.app/privacy
```

## Rules the copy follows
- Sentence case everywhere, including the description's section headers. Apple allows no formatting there, so each header sits on its own line with a blank line above it.
- No "AI" beyond the name; the product is a planner.
- Every claim maps to a shipped feature. Canvas sync, schedule updates, and overlap moves are Plus; the rest is free.
