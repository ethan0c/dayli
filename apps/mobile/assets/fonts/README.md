# Fonts

Geist Light, Regular, Medium and SemiBold (OFL), fetched from Google Fonts on Oct 19, 2026. Names match `packages/tokens` fontFamily.

Newsreader Regular (`Newsreader-Regular.ttf`, the 16pt optical-size static cut, OFL; license in `Newsreader-OFL.txt`), fetched from https://github.com/productiontype/Newsreader on Oct 20, 2026. It is the marketing voice, used only through the `displaySerif` token on the paywall tagline, the Plus welcome, the auth welcome and the two onboarding showcases. The website loads the same family from Google Fonts. The 16pt cut was chosen over the 72pt cut because its hairlines hold up at 34px on a phone.

# Gilroy preview

Downloaded from the designer's official free package on October 19, 2026:
https://tinkov.info/downloads/Gilroy-FREE.zip

The unmodified Light and ExtraBold OTF files and the package's Free Font EULA are included. This is the user's requested two-weight design preview. Body and medium tokens map to Light; semibold tokens map to ExtraBold. The website retains Onest.

The free EULA permits personal/commercial design and web use and restricts redistribution; it does not explicitly grant mobile app embedding. Resolve app embedding with the foundry before distributing a release. Regular, Medium, and Semibold are separate paid styles and are not included here.
